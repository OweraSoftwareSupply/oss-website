About Us

We approach software development as a trade. The mission is to provide the digital equivalent of heavy-duty hardware: tools that don't break, interfaces that don't confuse, and systems that stand the test of time.

Every task — whether a small utility plugin or a foundational gameplay feature — gets the same dedication and attention to detail, developed to specification without compromise.

Nolan Willis

FOUNDER & ENGINEER

An Unreal Engine developer with an M.S. in Game Programming and several years spent building editor tooling, gameplay systems, and UI. Started on a beat-up Nintendo 64, kept going through a Computer Science undergrad, and never stopped asking how games work.

The goal is to help bring stories to life: tools that streamline workflows, systems that expand what a game can do, and interfaces players actually want to use.

Our Cazenovia Roots

Owera Software Supply takes its name from Owera Point Road, a street on the north end of Cazenovia Lake. It is where a lot of time was spent growing up, and it is still home today.

Born on the shores of that lake, the company draws on the enduring quality of local hardware and the precision of nautical engineering. Software should be as dependable as a well-crafted tool.
